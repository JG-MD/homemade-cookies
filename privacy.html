Privacy Policy

1. Who is responsible?

Cookie Corner is a private, non-commercial cookie ordering platform operated for a closed group of friends. It is not a registered business. If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, contact the operator directly via the contact details you already have as a friend and member of this group.

2. What data we collect and why

We only collect data that is strictly necessary to operate the service:

  • Orders — your first name (or nickname), the cookie you ordered, size, quantity, and any optional note you leave. This is used solely to prepare and hand over your order.
  • Reviews — your first name (or any name you choose to enter), the cookie you reviewed, your star rating, and an optional comment. This is displayed publicly on the site so others can see what people thought.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, payment information, or any tracking or analytics data. We do not use cookies (the browser kind).

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

Processing your data is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — it is necessary to fulfil the purpose you submitted the data for (placing an order or leaving a review). For reviews that are displayed publicly, processing is additionally based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, given by voluntarily submitting the review form).

4. How long data is stored

  • Orders are kept for as long as needed to fulfil and track them. Once a batch is complete and all orders are handed over, old orders are deleted manually by the admin — typically within a few weeks of completion.
  • Reviews are kept as long as they are relevant to the site. You can request deletion at any time (see section 6).

There are no automated retention periods — data is managed manually by the site admin.

5. Who has access to your data

Only the site admin (the person running Cookie Corner) has access to all submitted data through a password-protected admin panel. Reviews are displayed publicly on the site as entered. No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

6. Third-party service: Supabase

All data is stored in a database provided by Supabase, Inc. (a US-based company operating under standard contractual clauses for GDPR compliance). Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf and has no independent right to use your data. You can review Supabase's privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that inaccurate data is corrected.
  • Deletion — request that your data is deleted ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — request that we stop actively processing your data.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent — for reviews, you can withdraw consent at any time, which means the review will be removed.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the operator directly. Requests will be handled within 30 days as required by law.

8. Usage guidelines

By using Cookie Corner you agree to the following:

  • You will not submit offensive, hateful, discriminatory, or illegal content in any form field.
  • You will not attempt to abuse, scrape, or overload the platform.
  • You will use the service only for its intended purpose — ordering cookies and leaving honest reviews.
  • You understand this is a private platform for a closed group, not a public commercial service.

The admin reserves the right to delete any order, review, or other content that violates these guidelines without prior notice.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes significantly, the "last updated" date at the top will be updated. Since this is a friends-only platform, changes will also be communicated directly.
